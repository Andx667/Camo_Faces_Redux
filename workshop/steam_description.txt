[h1]Camo Faces Redux[/h1]

[b]Camo Faces Redux[/b] (CFR) lets players camouflage their faces during Arma 3 missions. It's a rewrite of the original [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=346665985]Camofaces[/url] mod by Sk3y and Feldhobel.

[h1]Requirements[/h1]

[list]
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=450814997]CBA_A3[/url]
[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=463939057]ACE3[/url]
[/list]

[h1]Features[/h1]

Adds camo face variants for many of the base game's faces, in multiple schemes:

[list]
[*] Bundeswehr Camouflage
[*] Bundeswehr Stripes
[*] Serbian Camouflage
[*] Black (night camouflage)
[*] US Stripes
[*] US Flash
[*] US Stains
[*] Camo Variant of all Vanilla/Marksman DLC Faces
[/list]

Includes inventory items needed to apply each camo scheme, and a supply box stocked with all three.

[h1]How to Use[/h1]

Add a facepaint item to your uniform, then use ACE's self-interaction menu on yourself to open the Camo Faces dialog and apply or remove camouflage.

There's also a CBA setting to switch to a leaner, dialog-free flow: enable it and the self-interaction menu instead expands directly into one action per camo scheme, plus a remove action — no dialog required.

Includes a Compatibility for Zeus Enhanced to apply camo faces to players and AI units.
