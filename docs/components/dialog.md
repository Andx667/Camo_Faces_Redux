# Dialog (`cfr_dialog`)

The graphical interface for choosing and applying camouflage — see [Usage](../usage.md).

A CBA setting, **Use ACE Actions Instead of Dialog**, lets players skip the dialog entirely in favor of a flat ACE self-action per camo scheme.

Players can also **paint** or **clean** a friendly unit's face (player or AI) from the ACE interaction menu on that unit - see [Usage](../usage.md). A second CBA setting, **Allow Painting Other Units** (on by default, shared by all clients), turns this off.

Every layer of camo plays a short painting animation that other players see - a face wipe when you paint yourself, a reach forward when you paint or clean a teammate. A third CBA setting, **Enable Painting Animations** (on by default, each player's own choice unless the server or mission forces it), turns them off.

All textures used by the dialog were made by Sk3y and Feldhobel.
