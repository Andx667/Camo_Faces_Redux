# Faces (`cfr_faces`)

Adds the camouflaged face variants themselves.

Each base-game head (African, Asian, Greek, Persian, and White heads) gets camouflaged counterparts across the mod's schemes — BW Camouflage, BW Stripes, Night (Black), Serbian, US Stripes, US Stains, and US Flash — defined in `CfgFaces.hpp` and the per-ethnicity `Faces_*.hpp` files.

All textures and RVMATs were made by Sk3y and Feldhobel.
