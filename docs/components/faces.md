# Faces (`cfr_faces`)

Adds the camouflaged face variants themselves.

Each base-game head (African, Asian, Greek, Persian, and White heads) gets camouflaged counterparts across the mod's schemes — BW Camouflage, BW Stripes, Night (Black), Serbian, US Stripes, US Stains, US Flash, and Snow Stripes.

Heads added by later DLCs are covered too: Apex (the Tanoan heads and Asian 04-07), Contact (the Livonian and Russian heads, plus White 24-32) and Laws of War (Greek 11-14 and White 23). These appear only if you own the DLC in question, the same way the Vanilla scheme appears only for Marksmen owners. The Vanilla scheme is the one exception that doesn't extend to them, because its variants are Bohemia's own and only exist for the base game's original heads.

The named campaign faces Barklem, Mavros and Sturrock (Tac-Ops Mission Pack) and Ioannou (Tanks) are covered as well, and unlike the other DLC heads above they are always available regardless of who owns those DLCs - a mission can put any unit in one of these faces either way.

Marksmen also adds three environment-specific camo faces — arid, lush and semi-arid — which sit alongside Vanilla in the same category. Bohemia only made these for three faces, so they show up for `PersianHead_A3_01`, `GreekHead_A3_02` and `WhiteHead_11` and are hidden for everyone else.

The Night scheme isn't offered on the African or Tanoan heads, or on Barklem, where black paint on skin that dark would be all but invisible.

The camo schemes were designed and painted by Sk3y and Feldhobel.
