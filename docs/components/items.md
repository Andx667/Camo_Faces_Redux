# Items (`cfr_items`)

Adds the items needed to camouflage your face:

- **BW Facepaint** — used by the German Armed Forces
- **Serbian Facepaint Sticks** — used by the Serbian Armed Forces
- **US Facepaint Sticks** — used by the US Armed Forces
- **Box of Facepaint** — a supply crate stocked with all three, for mission makers to place

All models and textures were made by Sk3y and Feldhobel.
