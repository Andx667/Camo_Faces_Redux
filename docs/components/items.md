# Items (`cfr_items`)

Adds the items needed to camouflage your face:

| Item | Classname | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BW Facepaint Stick | `cfr_items_BW_FacepaintStick` | Used by the German Armed Forces. 10 uses |
| Serbian Facepaint Stick | `cfr_items_Serbian_FacepaintStick` | Used by the Serbian Armed Forces. 10 uses |
| US Facepaint Stick | `cfr_items_US_FacepaintStick` | Used by the US Armed Forces. 10 uses |
| Snow Stripes Facepaint Stick | `cfr_items_SnowStripes_FacepaintStick` | White/green paint for the Snow Stripes scheme. 10 uses |
| Box of Facepaint | `cfr_items_box` | A `Box_NATO_Support_F`-based supply crate stocked with 10 of each facepaint stick, for mission makers to place |

Each stick has **10 uses**: every time you camouflage your face - or paint a teammate's - through the dialog or the ACE actions, one use is spent, and the stick disappears when it is empty. Keep it anywhere in your uniform, vest or backpack; the number of uses left shows in the inventory and in a message after each application. Cleaning a face, and anything a Zeus or script does, is free.

The original plain facepaint items (`cfr_items_BW_Facepaint`, `cfr_items_Serbian_Facepaint`, `cfr_items_US_Facepaint`, `cfr_items_SnowStripes_Facepaint`) still work and never run out, so existing missions and loadouts keep working, but they are hidden from the arsenal and editor. Use the sticks in new content.

All models and textures were made by Sk3y and Feldhobel.
